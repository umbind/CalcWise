# Formula & Precision Standards

Every calculation in CalcWise must satisfy:
1. **Formula ID & Version:** Explicitly declared (e.g. `formula-percentage-v1.0.0`).
2. **Authority & Methodology:** Clear citation to academic, regulatory, or standard engineering sources.
3. **Internal Precision:** High precision internally; rounded strictly at presentation according to user/currency display rules.
4. **Golden Dataset:** Tested against established authoritative external calculations.
5. **Traceability:** Step-by-step mathematical representation provided in calculation trace.
